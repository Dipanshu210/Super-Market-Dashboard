# Super Market Dashboard

An interactive Power BI Business Intelligence dashboard designed to analyze and visualize retail sales data. This dashboard tracks key performance indicators (KPIs), operational metrics, and regional distributions to unlock actionable business insights, optimize supply chain metrics, and maximize profitability.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Metrics Captured
* **Total Performance Indicators**: 
  * Total Sales: **633K**
  * Quantity Sold: **10K**
  * Absolute Profit: **86K**
  * Growth Rate: **46.94%**
* **Geographical & Regional Performance**: Deep dive into sales distribution across regions (**West: 29%**, **East: 28%**, **Central: 24%**, **South: 18%**), accompanied by an interactive geographical map mapping profit and sales across states.
* **Temporal Sales Analysis**: Multi-year trend analysis (2014–2017) mapped by month and quarter to detect seasonality patterns and yearly growth trajectories.
* **Product Breakdown**: Detailed horizontal bar charts showing top performing product categories (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies) and sub-categories (Chairs, Phones, Machines).
* **Consumer Segments**: Sales share split by customer types consisting of **Consumers (47%)**, **Corporate (33%)**, and **Home Office (21%)**.

---

## 🛠️ Features Built-In
* **Interactive Slicers**: Dynamic regional filtering at the top right header (Central, East, South, West) enabling localized region-specific deep dives.
* **Time Intelligence Plots**: Comparative multi-line trend plots for month-over-month and quarter-over-quarter analysis.
* **Rich Geographic Mapping**: Data-point density map showing sales volumes across regional markets.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack
* **Tool**: Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Visualizations Used**: KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Stacked Bar Charts, Line Charts, Bubble Map, and Filter Slicers.
* **File Format**: `.pbit` / `.pbix`

---

## 🚀 How to Run the Project Locally
1. **Clone this repository** to your local machine.
2. Ensure you have **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** installed.
3. Download the raw dataset used for this project:
   * **Dataset Source:** [Kaggle Superstore Dataset by vivek468](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)
   * **File to download:** `Sample - Superstore.csv` (2.29 MB)
4. Open the `Super Market Dashboard.pbit` (or `.pbix`) file.
5. If prompted by Power BI to locate the missing data source, browse your files, select the downloaded `Sample - Superstore.csv` file, and click **Load/Refresh** to populate the visuals.
